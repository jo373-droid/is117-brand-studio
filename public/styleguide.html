00 / Direction

Why this
direction?

Decisions and audience needs recorded in SPEC.md.

Audience hypotheses, not research

Primary

A person using a simple shirt to express creative style.

Wants a more defined look while feeling loose; this is their preference, not a verified product claim.

Secondary

A person looking for an everyday basic.

Needs clear size and price information.

Direction

Postmodernist, playful, and contrarian.

06 / Voice & persuasion

Warm enough
to invite you.

Laid-back, contrarian, and clear.

Unity / self-identification

“If you like everyday pieces with a little edge, you’re in the right place.”

Lets a visitor decide whether this point of view fits their own taste. It does not claim that other customers agree.

Reciprocity / useful styling note

“Try the white tee with one bold layer or accessory you already own.”

A small, ungated idea gives the visitor something useful without making a product claim.

The demo uses fictional price and size data. It does not accept orders, payment, or personal information.
